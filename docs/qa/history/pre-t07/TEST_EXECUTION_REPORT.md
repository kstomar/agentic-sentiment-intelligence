blank-source regression and ten earlier source cases. T06 adds 110 cases in
four modules plus a synthetic helper. The two existing OpenAPI route inventory
checks were extended for authorized routes and distinct HMAC authentication;
no existing behavioral assertion was removed, weakened or skipped.

  duplicate headers, malformed signatures, unknown integration, expired/future

  concurrent duplicates, fresh timestamp retries and protected status/expired 404.

  concurrency, capacity rejection without active eviction, terminal retention
  exhaustion/expiry, lazy and periodic cleanup, deadline, provider/unexpected
  failures, active cancellation, pending drain and raw-reference cleanup.

  default 4 MiB boundaries, multibyte/emoji content, chunked bodies, absent and
